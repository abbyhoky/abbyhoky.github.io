## A system-level problem

AI-generated content farms are not only a question of whether one page contains an inaccurate statement. At scale, low-quality content can affect search experiences, information discovery and the material that may later be collected into datasets.

Search-engine optimisation and traffic monetisation can create incentives to publish large quantities of content, regardless of whether that content adds meaningful value.

## Responses need multiple layers

Possible responses include better content provenance, ranking and filtering approaches, platform accountability and stronger research into dataset quality. No single intervention is likely to address every part of the problem.

## Governance as shared responsibility

The issue connects technical design with platform incentives and public governance. Understanding those relationships is essential when considering what responsible interventions should look like.
