## Designing with an unsettled material

Generative AI is often discussed as a tool that can be selected and applied to a task. A design-material perspective invites a different question: how does the material shape the process, the designer and the possible outcome?

Its behaviour may be flexible, surprising and difficult to fully predict. Those qualities can become part of the design inquiry rather than simply problems to eliminate.

## Why speculate?

Speculative design can help make assumptions visible. By constructing possible scenarios and interactions, researchers can examine values, agency and responsibility before a technology becomes ordinary.

This does not replace empirical evaluation. Instead, it can complement it by helping teams ask better questions about the futures they are building toward.

## Keep the human consequences in view

The point is not to celebrate novelty. It is to understand how design choices distribute control, shape expectations and affect the people who encounter the resulting systems.
